# 💡 How to Learn Every Pattern

For each pattern:

- 📖 Learn the concept
- 🧠 Understand when to use it
- ✍️ Solve **5 Easy** problems
- 🔥 Solve **10 Medium** problems
- 🚀 Solve **2–3 Hard** problems
- ♻️ Revise after 1 day, 7 days, and 30 days

---

## ⭐ Pro Tip

> **Don't memorize solutions.**
>
> Learn to identify the **pattern** behind every problem.
>
> Once you recognize the pattern, the implementation becomes much easier.
